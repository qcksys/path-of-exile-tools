# Energy shield on block shield

The example builds overlapping flat-ES/percent-ES and flat-ES/block prefix pairs, then recombines them on a Shaper Titanium Spirit Shield with energy shield recovery on block. The percent-ES and block donors each include a non-native natural (NNN) evasion prefix from Weeping Essence of Doubt.

Revision 9 offers an optional production choice for each NNN donor. Both use the existing unrolled Normal level-86 Shaper Titanium Spirit Shield acquisition. Each production request buys its own item; the original prepared-donor purchases stay pinned until the user changes the choice. Earlier revisions keep their original graphs.

The shared Normal Titanium acquisition also offers a paid Scouring option. Its source acquisition offers three exact level-86 Shaper Titanium Spirit Shields: Magic with one T1 block prefix and no suffixes; Rare with the same isolated block prefix; or Magic with `LocalBaseEnergyShieldAndMana3` and `RecoverLifePercentOnBlockUber1_`. Each source has no crafted or fractured modifier. One Orb of Scouring removes its explicit modifiers and preserves the canonical base, level, Shaper influence and other item state. Each request buys a fresh item and pays for Scouring; the original Normal purchase and original Magic source stay selected by default. The additional alternatives start unpriced and require their own compatible captured quotes or manual prices.

The route applies Weeping Essence of Doubt, then Annuls until only its forced evasion prefix remains. It uses an Exalted Orb to attempt the required T1 percent-ES or block prefix. An unwanted added modifier is Annulled: retaining the NNN prefix alone returns to Exalted Orbs, while losing NNN triggers Scouring and another Essence on the same base. All attempts are charged, including failed isolation, Exalted Orbs and resets. Recovery does not buy another base.

Weeping Doubt limits its random modifier pool to level 60, so it cannot directly roll these level-86 native prefixes. The item itself stays level 86, allowing the later Exalted Orb to roll them. Scouring is necessary before reapplying this low-tier Essence, which requires a Normal item.

Each preparation returns a Rare shield with exactly two prefixes and no suffixes: the original canonical native modifier and the evasion modifier with its essence provenance preserved. Numeric values are sampled within their canonical ranges. The base, level, Shaper influence, quality, implicits and flags are preserved; the original purchase and final shield requirements are unchanged.

Prices for the base, Weeping Essence of Doubt, Orb of Annulment, Exalted Orb and Orb of Scouring must be supplied before a complete preparation cost is available. A missing input keeps the total unknown. This route does not establish current listing availability, change simulation limits or promise a completed estimate within the initial preview budget. A completed single trial proves its own spending, not an expected crafting cost.

The flat-ES/recovery donor also offers **Roll the Magic flat ES and recovery pair with Alterations**. It uses the same Normal Shaper Titanium source, Transmutes once, then pays for Alterations until both `LocalIncreasedEnergyShield10` and `RecoverEnergyShieldPercentOnBlockUber1` appear together. Misses reroll the same item. If Transmutation supplies both modifiers, the Alteration step is skipped.

This preparation returns the exact one-prefix, one-suffix Magic state already offered by the donor's Magic purchase alternative. Its recombination outcomes and recovery behavior are equivalent to the original Rare purchase, which stays selected by default. It does not silently change the purchased item or require a Regal/Annul conversion. It can also consume the explicit Scouring option; each request buys and Scours a fresh item before rolling.

The base, Transmutation and Alteration prices are required, with Scouring added when that source option is selected. Missing inputs remain unknown. Rolling both modifiers can require many attempts; this is a faithful optional route, not a claim that it is cheapest or will finish within the initial preview budget. Default calculation limits remain unchanged.

The life-on-block shield example offers the same explicit Scouring choice for its unrolled bases. A Magic level-86 Shaper Heat-attuned Tower Shield with one `Strength7` suffix and no prefixes can supply both its block and maximum-resistance preparations. A Magic level-86 Shaper Pinnacle Tower Shield with one `DisplaySocketedGemsGetReducedReservationUber2` suffix and no prefixes supplies its recovery preparation. The two Heat choices share a source definition and Scouring step, but consuming both buys two items and pays twice. All original purchases remain pinned.

These ordinary purchases require exact canonical modifiers and affix counts, level, influence and supported flags. They do not add an exact native implicit roll or socket count to the original requirements. Historical asks are not filled in as prices; each Magic source needs a compatible captured quote or an explicit manual price.

Revision 12 also offers Magic/Rare sources with disposable explicit modifiers directly on the four Normal shield acquisitions. Each source must prove its exact level-86 Shaper base, native implicits, absence of fractures and other incompatible persistent state. The four price profiles distinguish crafted modifiers and enchantments: remove crafts for one Scouring when present, remove enchantments for three Scourings when present, then spend one Scouring on the explicit modifiers. Their individual identities need not be invented to establish that they can be removed. The source ask and every preparation input are charged separately, once for each acquired item. These alternatives retain the original Normal output and default selection, and do not duplicate the existing concrete-source Scouring step.

The three ordinary Hubris donor acquisitions in the rarity-helmet preset use the same paid preparation with an uninfluenced level-86 Hubris Circlet. This does not supply its separate Janus donor. Source definitions establish eligibility; compatible observed quotes or explicit manual prices are still required.
