# Triple energy shield chest preparation

The r9 preset offers optional preparation for its Unfaltering and Seraphim's
donors. The original purchases remain selected by default. Earlier revisions keep
their existing graphs, and choosing preparation does not change the final chest
target or simulation settings.

Both alternatives reuse the existing uninfluenced Normal item-level-86 Vaal
Regalia acquisition. Each consumed donor buys its own base, even though the source
node and price are shared. No new purchase definition or market cohort is needed.

| Donor | Required native prefix | Non-native natural prefix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unfaltering | `LocalIncreasedEnergyShieldPercent8` | `LocalIncreasedEvasionRating8` |
| Seraphim's | `LocalIncreasedEnergyShieldPercentAndStunRecovery6` | `LocalIncreasedEvasionRating8` |

Select **Roll the ES prefix with Screaming Doubt, then isolate** on either donor
to use its two paid crafting steps:

1. Apply Screaming Essence of Doubt until the required ES prefix appears alongside
   the guaranteed evasion prefix. Screaming Doubt accepts both Normal and Rare
   items and rolls additional modifiers at the actual item level of 86.
2. Use Orbs of Annulment until exactly those two prefixes and no suffixes remain.
   If either required prefix is removed, apply another Screaming Doubt to the same
   Rare item. Every Essence and Annulment is charged, including unsuccessful attempts.

The result is a Rare Vaal Regalia with the original base, item level, influences
and other item state. The evasion prefix retains its Essence provenance. Actual
rolled values remain within the canonical modifier ranges; preparation does not
substitute a different tier or invent a specific roll.

Each alternative requires a compatible base price, Screaming Doubt price and
Annulment price. A missing component leaves the total unknown. Prices are not
filled from historical individual listings, and no current market availability is
implied by the preset. The existing flat-ES donor still uses its separate
Alteration preparation option. Recombination and its existing recovery routes are
unchanged.
