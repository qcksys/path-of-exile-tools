Regroup PoE 1 market items to match the trade site's exchange groups. Runegrafts and Kalguuran runes move from Currency to a new Runes category, Enshrouding Crystals to a new Legion category, and Astragali, Exotic Coinage, Scrap Metal and Burial Medallion to a new Expedition category. Timeless splinters, Crescent Splinter and Memory of Trauma move from Currency to Fragments. Scrying Orbs move to Maps, and a `scrying-orb` signature splits them into one market per map, named by the orb's `Map Area` property.
