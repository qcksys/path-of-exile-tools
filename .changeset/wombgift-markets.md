Split PoE 1 Wombgift markets by item level, since birthed items take the Wombgift's item level. A `wombgift` signature puts each Wombgift in one of five bands: 1–67, 68–74, 75–82, 83 and 84+. These are poe.ninja's bands, except that its 75–83 band is split so tier 16 map drops (item level 83) are priced apart from lower map drops.
