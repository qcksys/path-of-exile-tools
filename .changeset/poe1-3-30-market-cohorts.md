PoE 1 market cohorts, the capture policy and the crafting base market list are generated from the 3.30 catalog (r18), so listings are matched by 3.30 modifier text, tiers and native implicit rolls (Two-Toned Boots now roll +16–20%). Cord Belt replaces Mechalarm Belt among the tracked belt bases: Mechalarm Belt cohorts stop receiving hours and leave current price lookups once their last hour is more than a day old. Capture also accepts Enshrouded Quiver, Mycelium Core and the Jun veiled jewel modifiers.
