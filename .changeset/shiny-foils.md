Merge foil and non-foil listings of an item into one season market. The market list, the item page's variants and its price history combine them, and the "Foil" labels are gone. The market filters drop the `foil` parameter (old links that carry it open the merged market), and market rows from the HTTP and MCP market operation no longer include `foilVariation`.
