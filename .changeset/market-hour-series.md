Stash market item charts now show min, interquartile and average asks and likely sales, with an interquartile band only in hours with more than 8 observations and average asks hidden until selected in the legend. The count panel adds delisted listings, and a new panel shows the average age of likely sales. The ingest workers now record that age as `soldAgeSeconds`, stored in `stash_<game>_market_hour` and returned with market hours over HTTP and MCP; earlier hours have none. Exchange item charts add the lowest rate and an interquartile range estimated from each hour's lowest, highest and volume-weighted rates, returned as `p25` and `p75` by `get_season_exchange_item`. The Forbidden jewels tab sorts by pair price and likely sales, and charts each jewel's recent likely sales. Stash market item charts also shade the interquartile range of delisted listings' last asks in hours with more than 8 of them. The ingest workers now record those prices as `delistedPrices`, stored in `stash_<game>_market_hour` and returned with market hours over HTTP and MCP along with `mergedDelisted`; earlier hours have none. The item chart's tooltip now shows one hour across all its panels: a compact table of that hour's prices in one currency, then its counts.
