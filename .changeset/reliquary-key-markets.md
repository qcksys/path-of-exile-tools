Capture PoE 1 Reliquary Keys (`Cosmic Reliquary Key`, `Ancient Reliquary Key` and the other Vault Keys) from public stashes. Apart from the Voidborn key, which has a Currency frame and was already captured, they are Normal items, so the currency rule missed them and they had no stash price. Each key is keyed by its base type and listed on the market under Fragments.
