Add buy-in prices to crafting projects. The new poe.boats Trade Pricing browser extension (Chrome and Firefox) runs each craft step's official trade search in your own browser and pathofexile.com session, paced by the trade site's rate limits; poe.boats never sees the session. Listings are re-checked against each step, priced at the cheapest matching instant-buyout listing (in-person listings are opt-in), and saved with the project. The Buy-in prices table compares buying at each step with crafting from scratch by expected cost per finished item, and the same pricing and comparison are available over HTTP and MCP. Generated trade searches now default to instant-buyout listings.
