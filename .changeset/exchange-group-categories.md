PoE 1 market categories now come from each base item's ID, item class and tags rather than its name, checked against the trade site's exchange groups. New Allflame embers (both kinds of ember) and Ducats & charts categories. Loyalty, Honoured, Ancestral and Forbidden tattoos move to Tattoos; Astrolabes, Breach Blessings, Breach and Timeless splinters, Simulacrum and Ritual splinters, Ritual Vessel, Valdo's Puzzle Box and every Memory thread to Fragments; Expedition artifacts to Expedition; Enshrouded items to Legion; Oil Extractor to Oils; Valdo, Vaal Temple and Shaper Guardian maps to Maps. The Catalyst divination card, Veiled Scarab and Maven's Chisel of Scarabs no longer land in Catalysts or Scarabs. When bases share a name, the released tradeable one decides the category. Public stash ingest now captures area level 83 Heist contracts, split by the job they require (not its level), and blueprints with one or every wing revealed, split by wings revealed and enchants.
