Sort most of the PoE 1 market's Other items into categories. Necropolis corpses are listed under Corpses (Crafting), and a new League mechanics group lists Heist (contracts, blueprints, targets and Heist gear), Wombgifts, and Encounter items (Chronicle of Atzoatl, Inscribed Ultimatum, Forbidden Tome, Sanctum research, Expedition Logbook and similar map device items). Offering to the Goddess is listed under Fragments, and the four spirit beasts (Farrul, Craiceann, Fenumus and Saqawal) under Beasts. Enshrouded items stay in Other.
