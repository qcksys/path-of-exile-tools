Show 24-hour and 3-day price changes beside the 7-day one. Exchange items and stash markets list all three under their price, and their item pages chart each window's prices beside its change. Exchange changes are in the exchange unit; stash changes follow the selected price currency, so an item listed at a steady divine price shows no change in divine while divine rises against chaos. `get_season_market` adds `hourly`, `change24h` and `change3d` to `exchange` rows (and `get_season_exchange_item` to its `item`), `priceTrend` to `rows`, and `priceTrend` for an item.
