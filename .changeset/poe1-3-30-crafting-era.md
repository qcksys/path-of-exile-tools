Crafting uses PoE 1 3.30 data from client 3.30.0.2.2 as a new 3.30 era (revision r18); projects on 3.29 keep r17. Harvest crafts charge every lifeforce type the client lists, including Crystallised Rancour. Granted skill and support lines such as "Grants Level 20 Aspect of the Cat" render, match trade text and export to PoB with the client's 3.30 wording. Fishing rod lure and hook modifiers are named from the client's lure and hook tables, which 3.30 uses in place of per-value text. Presets pick the prefix or suffix lock modifier the bench offers on their item class. The new 1 or 2 Prefix or Suffix Modifiers cannot be changed crafts are refused until their protection is modelled.
